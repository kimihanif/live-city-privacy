Privacy Policy for Tamil Muslim Calendar

Effective date: June 24, 2026

Tamil Muslim Calendar (the "App") is developed and maintained by Hanif. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

The App has no user account and no login, and it does not collect personal information automatically — no name, phone number, precise location, or advertising identifiers. The only personal information the App ever receives is what you choose to send us through the in-app feedback form (an optional email address, plus the message and any screenshot you submit) — see "Feedback you send" below. To understand how the App is used and to improve it, the App also collects anonymous usage analytics — see "Analytics and session replay" below.

Network requests

All calendar data is bundled inside the App, so the calendar itself works offline: no network connection is needed to view dates, festivals, or reminders. The App sends data over the network in only two cases: anonymous usage analytics (described below), and feedback that you actively choose to submit (described below). Nothing else is transmitted, and neither is required to use the calendar.

Analytics and session replay

To understand which features people use and to improve the App, we use PostHog, a third-party product-analytics service, as a data processor acting on our behalf. Analytics data is sent to and stored on PostHog servers located in the European Union.

The data we collect is anonymous and is not linked to your identity. It consists of:

  • Usage events — anonymous records of actions in the App, such as which month you view, opening a day or holiday detail, zooming, or turning a reminder on or off.
  • Session replays — recordings of your on-screen interactions within the App (the calendar screens shown and where you tap), used to find usability problems. The App shows only public calendar content and has no login or accounts; any text you type (such as feedback) is masked automatically in session replays.
  • Device and app information — such as the App version, device model and manufacturer, operating system and version, screen language, and time zone.
  • A random anonymous identifier generated on your device so that events from the same installation can be grouped together. It is not your name, email, advertising ID, or any account, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App. Your approximate location (country or region only) may be inferred from your IP address by the analytics provider; no precise or GPS location is ever collected.

We do not sell this data and do not use it for advertising. For details on how PostHog handles data, see posthog.com/privacy.

Feedback you send

The App includes an optional in-app feedback form for sending a feature request, improvement idea, or bug report. It is used only when you choose to — you are never required to send feedback.

When you submit feedback, the App sends the following to our backend processor, Appwrite, where it is stored on Appwrite Cloud servers located in Singapore:

  • the message you write and the feedback type (feature, improvement, or bug);
  • an optional email address, only if you enter one, used solely to reply to you — leave it blank to remain anonymous;
  • an optional screenshot, only if you attach one;
  • basic technical context to make the report actionable — the App version and edition, your device model, platform, operating-system version, and a timestamp.

Feedback submissions are anonymous: there is no account, and no advertising or analytics identifier is attached to them. To attach a screenshot, the App opens your device's photo picker so you can select one image; on Android 13 and newer this uses the system photo picker, which needs no photo or storage permission, and the App does not access your camera. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message or screenshots. We use feedback only to understand issues and improve the App; we do not sell it or use it for advertising. You can ask us to delete feedback you have sent by emailing the contact address below. For how Appwrite handles data, see appwrite.io/privacy.

Information stored on your device

The App stores small amounts of data locally on your device: your settings preferences (such as whether festival reminders are turned on), and the anonymous analytics identifier together with a short-lived queue of analytics events waiting to be sent. Your reminder preferences are never transmitted off your device. All locally stored data is removed when you uninstall the App.

Notifications and reminders

The App can show local reminder notifications — week-ahead and month-ahead digests of upcoming festivals, holidays, and new-moon (crescent) dates — that are built and scheduled entirely on your device from the calendar data bundled in the App. The App does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging or any server to send these reminders, and no device token or personal data is generated, stored, or transmitted. On Android 13 and newer the App asks for notification permission first; you can deny it, or turn reminders off at any time in your device settings.

Permissions

The App requests the following permission:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — requested on Android 13+ so the App can show local reminder notifications on your device. You are prompted before any notification is shown and can deny or revoke this permission at any time. The App does not require internet access to show the calendar.

The App does not request access to your camera, contacts, microphone, or location.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. It has no accounts and collects personal information only if someone actively chooses to send feedback, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will always reflect the most recent version.
