Privacy Policy for Malayalam Muslim Calendar

Effective date: June 17, 2026

Malayalam Muslim Calendar (the "App") is developed and maintained by Hanif. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

Information we collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no user account, no login, and no in-app analytics or advertising.

Network requests

The App works fully offline. All calendar data is bundled inside the App, so it makes no network requests and sends no data off your device in order to display the calendar.

Information stored on your device

The App saves a small settings preference — such as whether festival reminders are turned on — locally on your device. This preference never leaves your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

Notifications

If you turn on festival reminders, the App schedules local notifications that are created and shown entirely on your device. The App does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging or any server to send these reminders, and no device token or personal data is generated, stored, or transmitted. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

Permissions

The App requests the following permission:

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — requested on Android 13+ only if you enable festival reminders, so the App can display them on your device. You are prompted before any notification is shown and can deny or revoke this permission at any time. The App does not require internet access to show the calendar.

Children's privacy

The App is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above will always reflect the most recent version.
